  • 8.5 oz./yd² (US) 14.1 oz./L yd (CA), 80/20 ring-spun cotton/polyester blend fleece with 100% cotton face, 20 singles

The fabric color of this 8.5 oz., 2-end fleece full-zip hooded sweatshirt is a close match to our 3-end fleece bottoms. If you are using this sweatshirt to color match IND20PNT/IND20SRT as a set, please order samples to see how well they match.

Please note: The heather colorways are not made of 100% cotton face yarn. Please do your own testing prior to screen printing.
